  • Side Step (Active) Once per round, may perform Side Step maneuver and suffer a number of strain to upgrade difficulty of all incoming ranged attacks by an equal number for the next round. Strain suffered this way cannot exceed ranks in Side Step.
  • Conditioned (Passive) Remove per rank of Conditioned from Athletics and Coordination check. Reduce the damage and strain suffered from falling by 1 per rank of Conditioned.
  • Street Smarts (Passive) Remove per rank of Street Smarts from Streetwise or Knowledge (Underworld) checks.
  • Reflect ×2 (Active) When hit by a ranged attack, suffer 3 strain to reduce damage by 2 plus ranks in Reflect.
