# The Mandalorian Clans

*A standing power — the one people who never demobilized.*

## Who they are

Rebuilt under a Mandalore into clan companies that hire out to anyone whose bond clears — which, in a disarmed galaxy, makes them the finest soldiers alive and everyone's contingency plan. They remember the wars differently than everyone else: **as work.**

## On the Awakening

They hold the era's strangest position, which is no position at all — the clans take foundlings from every ruin they are paid to make or guard, and a foundling who bends luck is simply a promising warrior.

**Nobody is watching the one culture that neither fears the gifted nor wants to own them.**
