◆ Table notes

Kinch — table notes

Concept. A Jedi training droid, two hundred years on Coruscant, who always knew the nice children were the wrong target. Hammer-sees-nails: offering to fight someone is, sincerely, a friendly gesture — it is what he was built for and did for centuries. Plays for comedy. Punches; does little else.

The build (second pass). The "multiclass droid" route to a Crit 1 punch: Steel Hand Adept plus Martial Artist for Iron Body ×4, which takes the standard Brawl critical rating from 5 to 1 — and since a Brawl weapon augments an unarmed attack (core rules; Sam Stewart's ruling), the vamblades swing at that rating too. Paid for with Brawn 5 → 2 and three bought Brawl ranks: the pool stays 2 yellow + 3 green, the damage does not (fists 2, blades 3).

How he fights (the player's own notes)

  • Up to four hits a round: a crit on a hit (1 Advantage now), then Precision Strike (Improved) (2 strain) turns it into Overpowered — a free second attack with the same pool — and 2 Advantage on each attack lands the off-hand vamblade as well.
  • Discouraging Wound via Precision Strike (1 strain): landed on an NPC, flips a dark-side Destiny Point to light. He seizes fate by the throat.
  • Martial Grace (2 strain): adds his Coordination ranks to the next Brawl hit — worth nothing until he buys Coordination.
  • Against vehicles with armour 0 he can crit with his fists once a hit deals damage (10 damage after the ÷10 — four Successes); armour 1 stops him cold. He has no Gunnery.
  • Warrior duel option, at the GM's discretion: forgo the initiative roll for an Average Perception or Knowledge check for a boost die against one chosen enemy — the stare-down.
  • Took the +10 Obligation options (+5 XP, +1,000 credits) — both spent.

Rules check (GM)

  • Crit 1 is legitimate now (Iron Body ×4: 5 − 4 = 1), for fists and for the vamblades alike. A crit costs 1 Advantage; the four-hit round needs 3 Advantage on the first roll (1 for the crit, 2 for the off-hand).
  • But a crit needs the hit to deal damage. Damage is 3 (blades) or 2 (fists) plus net Successes, against soak 3–5 on most of the Run's enforcers: he needs 1–3 net Successes before the crit can fire at all. Brawn 2 is the price of Crit 1; it is a real price.
  • Brawl rank 5 at character creation breaks the creation cap (no skill above rank 2 at creation). Two ranks are free (career + spec); the three bought ranks — 60 XP — are the problem. Ian's call: waive the cap, or send those 60 XP back to Brawn (2 → 3 is 30) and toughness.
  • The Jedi Training Suit is not equipped in this export (soak 3 instead of 5). Probably an accident of the rebuild; worn armour costs 3 less encumbrance, so it fits under his threshold of 7 alongside the vamblades.
  • The Obligation lost its name and text in the re-export (it is a bare 10 with the options still ticked) and the Purpose motivation is gone; the comlink went too. Re-enter Contract — the Lamplighters' Guild and Purpose: "I was made to punch — what?"; with the four-PC base of 10 the Obligation should carry 20.
  • No Force Rating for a droid despite the Warrior career; the Steel Hand tree's Force talents are closed to him. The sheet prints none.
  • Free career ranks: the app allowed six; a droid Warrior gets 3 + 2 = five. One rank over.

◆ Background

Kinch spent 200 years as a jedi training droid on Coruscant, always knowing there had to be a better target for his violence than the nice children he was tasked with fighting. One day he heard a master say, "sometimes, one must take destiny into their own hands" and he had an epiphany - later that night, Kinch cornered the master alone and unarmed, and left him covered in Discouraging Wounds before leaping out of a window into a speeder which was passing below by "chance". Ever since that night, Kinch has seized fate by the throat in pursuit of proper opponents.
