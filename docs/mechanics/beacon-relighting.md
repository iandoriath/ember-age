# Relighting a Beacon

*The set-piece skill challenge, built so different PCs shine. In play the relight itself is the Wake-it stage run as three stations; Chart it is the jump that got you there, and Speak to it is the certificate afterward.*

## The three stages

### 1. Wake it

**Mechanics or Computers** against sixty years of dead-man failsafes. Default: **Average Mechanics or Computers** per station; a module sets harder work where the mast earns it (Hard Mechanics to sequence stacks that have never fired).

### 2. Chart it

**Hard Astrogation**, with [fragments](lore-fragments.md) and rutters as boost dice.

### 3. Speak to it

A **Knowledge or social check** to satisfy old Republic handshake protocols — or to convince a guild observer to certify the lighting. Default: **Average Negotiation or Charm** with a certifier present; **Hard Knowledge (Education)** without one, cold against the protocols.

## The relight rule

A relight is **three stations over three rounds**. Every station needs its own hands; cut between them like a heist. Every round past the second adds a setback die at every station (the tide, the ash, the pickets: whatever the module names as the clock). Any station not green at the end of round three fires the beacon *wrong*: run the module's mast-fires-early paragraph. A module may say otherwise (Verdanth's relay is easy: no stations, no clock).

"Stage" here and "station" in the modules are the same unit, so the house spends in [Reading the Dice](dice-results.md) that name "beacon relight, any stage" apply per station.

## What a relit beacon does

Each relit beacon:

- **Opens a region** — a corridor of worlds ninety years strange.
- **Shifts the faction map.**
- **Pays a first-lighting fee** (see [the Lamplighters](../factions/lamplighters.md)).
- **Raises the same question: who follows you through?**
