◆ Table notes

Kinch — table notes

Concept. A Jedi training droid, two hundred years on Coruscant, who always knew the nice children were the wrong target. Hammer-sees-nails: offering to fight someone is, sincerely, a friendly gesture — it is what he was built for and did for centuries. Plays for comedy. Punches; does little else.

How he fights (the player's own notes)

  • Up to four hits a round: a crit on a hit, then Precision Strike (Improved) (2 strain) turns it into Overpowered — a free second attack with the same pool — and 2 Advantage on each attack lands the off-hand vamblade as well.
  • Discouraging Wound via Precision Strike (1 strain): flips a Destiny Point. He seizes fate by the throat.
  • Against vehicles with no armour (or armour 1, on a big roll) he can crit with his fists — picture him ripping out exposed hydraulics or fuel lines. He has no Gunnery; this is legitimately his best option.
  • Warrior duel option, at the GM's discretion: forgo the initiative roll (take a slot as if he rolled nothing) to make an Average Perception or Knowledge check for a boost die against one chosen enemy — the Qui-Gon/Maul stare-down.
  • Took the +10 Obligation options (+5 XP, +1,000 credits) — both spent: Brawn 5, the paired vamblades.

Rules check (GM)

  • Crit rating is 3, not 1. Iron Body ×2 takes unarmed crits from 5 to 3 and does not touch the vamblades (printed crit 3). Triggering a crit costs 3 Advantage, so the four-hit round needs 5 Advantage on one roll (3 for the crit + 2 for the off-hand). Still very live with Brawn 5 and Accurate 1; just not on 1 Advantage.
  • No Force Rating. Droids cannot have one; the Warrior career's FR 1 does not apply, and the Steel Hand tree's Force talents (Acklay's Scything Strike, Sapith Sundering, Far Strike, Force Rating) are closed to him. The sheet prints none.
  • Free career ranks: the app allowed six (all six Warrior skills); a droid Warrior gets 3 + 2 = 5. One rank over — pick one to drop, or let it stand.
  • Obligation reads 10 in the export — that is the +10 from the options alone. With the four-PC base of 10, his line should carry 20 (Contract: the Lamplighters' Guild).

◆ Background

Kinch spent 200 years as a jedi training droid on Coruscant, always knowing there had to be a better target for his violence than the nice children he was tasked with fighting. One day he heard a master say, "sometimes, one must take destiny into their own hands" and he had an epiphany - later that night, Kinch cornered the master alone and unarmed, and left him covered in Discouraging Wounds before leaping out of a window into a speeder which was passing below by "chance". Ever since that night, Kinch has seized fate by the throat in pursuit of proper opponents.
