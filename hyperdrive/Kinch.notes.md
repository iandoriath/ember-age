# Kinch — table notes

**Concept.** A Jedi training droid, two hundred years on Coruscant, who always knew the nice children were the wrong target. Hammer-sees-nails: offering to fight someone is, sincerely, a friendly gesture — it is what he was built for and did for centuries. Plays for comedy. Punches; does little else.

**How he fights**

- **Crit 1.** Four ranks of Iron Body take the Brawl critical rating from 5 to 1, and a Brawl weapon augments an unarmed attack, so the vamblades swing at 1 too. A crit costs **1 Advantage** (or a Triumph).
- **Four hits a round.** A crit on a hit, then *Precision Strike (Improved)* (2 strain, once per round) turns it into **Overpowered** — a free second attack with the same pool — and 2 Advantage on each attack lands the off-hand vamblade as well. The full round needs 3 Advantage on the first roll: 1 for the crit, 2 for the off-hand.
- **A crit needs the hit to deal damage.** Damage is 3 (blades) or 2 (fists) plus net Successes; against soak 3–5 that means 1–3 net Successes before the crit can fire.
- **Discouraging Wound** via *Precision Strike* (1 strain, any time he crits): landed on an NPC, flips a dark-side Destiny Point to light. He seizes fate by the throat.
- ***Martial Grace*** (2 strain): adds his Coordination ranks to the next Brawl hit — worth nothing until he buys Coordination.
- **Vehicles.** Personal-scale damage is divided by ten against a vehicle and a crit still needs the hit to deal damage: against armour 0 he needs four Successes to scratch it, then 1 Advantage for the crit. Armour 1 stops fists cold. He has no Gunnery.
- **The stare-down** (GM's discretion): forgo the initiative roll — take a slot as if he rolled nothing — for an Average Perception or Knowledge check that grants a boost die against one chosen enemy.
- **Droid.** No Force Rating and no Force talents, ever; the Steel Hand tree's Force nodes are closed to him. Repaired with Mechanics and repair patches, not medicine. Immune to poison, vacuum, and mind-altering Force powers.
