# Obligation, Reflavored

*Roll or choose. Everyone takes one — it is a broke, indebted galaxy.*

| d10 | Obligation | The Ember Age version |
|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | **Debt** | Sector scrip or a Kajidic marker, ruinous interest |
| 2 | **Family** | You are an isolated homeworld's only link |
| 3 | **Oath** | To a dying archivist |
| 4 | **Notice** | An Admiralty conscription writ with your name on it |
| 5 | **Secret** | Someone knows you are Force-sensitive — or love someone who is |
| 6 | **Obsession** | One fragment you cannot let go |
| 7 | **Responsibility** | Refugees from a lane that died |
| 8 | **Contract** | The Lamplighters hold your ship's papers |
| 9 | **Posted** | A Guild bounty, still live, with your name on it |
| 10 | Choose or invent | Keep it era-true: debts, memory, and the people a dead lane stranded |

Run Obligation as written in the core rules (starting totals, triggers, strain effects at threshold). The reflavoring changes who comes knocking, not the math.
