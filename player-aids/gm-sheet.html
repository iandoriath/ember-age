4 Opposed & assistance

  • Opposed: purples = higher of target's skill/characteristic, upgraded a number of times = the lower.
  • Skilled assist (unstructured / GM call): one roll with the best characteristic AND best skill across both helpers.
  • Unskilled assist: add instead (GM caps stacking).
  • In combat: Assist is a maneuver — engaged helper grants .
  • No re-rolls: a new attempt needs a new approach or a new cost.

6 Adversaries

  • Minion group: WT = n × one WT · strain = wounds · group skills at n−1 ranks · one drops per WT multiple; any crit drops one.
  • Rival: full skills; no strain track (strain = wounds).
  • Nemesis: both tracks; Adversary X = upgrade difficulty of combat checks against them X times.
  • Improv: minion 1–2 chars at 3 · rival 2–3 · nemesis 3–4 + Adversary 1–2.

8 Fear

Discipline (or Cool) vs situational difficulty — unsettling up to supernatural . Fail: on everything until end of encounter. Threat: strain. Despair: +1 difficulty on everything until end of encounter. Severe failures may flee/freeze (GM call). Force-sensitive fail: optionally Conflict = check difficulty, penalties still apply.

9 Destiny points

Session start: 1 Force die per player — Light pips → player pool, Dark → GM pool. Spending flips the point to the other side. Players: upgrade a PC check → · upgrade difficulty of an NPC check · buy a helpful fact · power talents. GM: mirror image against the PCs.

10 The Ember Age — house rules

  • Fragments: a held, relevant fragment = on related Knowledge. Tags Jedi/Sith/Civic — same event, different tags, one load-bearing disagreement. Always.
  • Reconstruction: 3+ fragments on one Question → Hard Knowledge (Lore), group scene. All three tags: upgrade ability once. One tag only: upgrade difficulty once. Success = Truth + Insight (remove a Setback category, or unlock an out-of-career talent) · Triumph = a secret beyond the Question · Despair = a corrupted conclusion, believed.
  • Witnesses: open Force use → community shifts one step (awe or fear) + on social checks there until addressed.
  • Wellsprings: Force Rating up / new power tree = XP plus a Wellspring found in play (holocron, vergence, living mentor).
  • Relight a beacon (3 stages, any order): Wake it Mechanics/Computers vs dead-man failsafes · Chart it Hard Astrogation, fragments = · Speak to it Knowledge or social vs old Republic handshake protocols, or convince the guild observer.

11 Session spine

XP (house): 15–20 per session, +5 for strong Obligation play. And the line of the era, worth saying once a night: "Nobody broke the galaxy — everyone just stopped holding it up."
