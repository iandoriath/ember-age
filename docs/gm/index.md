## Running the era — one-paragraph reminders

- **Nobody broke the galaxy** — everyone just stopped holding it up. No villain behind the Withering galaxy-wide; keep it that way. The one exception is local and locked: **this region withered because the Sith required time for their searches** — its lights were helped out under the Ebb's cover and kept out ([Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md)). The era is nobody's fault; this country was somebody's project.
- **History can still be interviewed** — and the interviews disagree. Every big fact should arrive twice, contradicting itself once.
- **The Force is an event, not a skill.** Every open use has witnesses, and witnesses have funerals behind them.
- **Hope with rust on it.** The era rewards repair, not conquest. Let things be fixable — at a price.
- **Pacing.** The prose runs elegiac, so schedule the comic engines deliberately (Old Gormo's eel, Marrow's wager, the court at Enarc, Hesk Varrow and Old Mim) and alternate heavy and light; after two sessions that end in paperwork, the third ends in weather.

## Terms you will meet in the modules

One line each, with the file that owns the definition.

- **The Record** — the certificate of relight's blank line, *Lighting party: ______*, and everything the crew swear to it. The first paper of the reopened corridor; everyone who comes up the lane after them reads it, and what they put on it travels ahead of them. ([Module 01](modules/01-the-light-on-vesta-9.md), Scene 6.)
- **The Scrub** — the Sith line's real work on the Run: every genuine Sith record removed, every fake left on its shelf, so the galaxy is left *wrong*, not ignorant. Two phases: the catalogued vaults swept ahead of the light, and whatever the diggers turn up taken behind it. It comes through the stone, never the seal. ([Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md), the machinations.)
- **The Weeding** — whenever the relighting stalls, the obstacle removes itself: a rival withdraws, an official retires young, a rutter goes missing. One learnable tell: the obstacle vanishes the night after a guild packet leaves port. It also guards the Dead Gate. ([Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md), the machinations.)
- **The Census** — the true line's watch on the gifted, and quietly Veritis's search for an ear that can hear the Muster; the Inheritors' harvested Vesta-9 file completes it. The Inheritors' own *Vesta-9 census* is a different paper: the clerk's file on the crew, filed in Session One. ([Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md), the machinations; [Vigil & Inheritors](npcs/vigil-inheritors.md).)
- **The Patronage** — the coreward "antiquarian" front through which the line funds the Lamplighters, on two conditions: the relighting proceeds in the patron's sequence, and the patron's survey teams get first look. The sequence is the search pattern; the survey teams are the Scrub. ([Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md).)
- **The whisper network** — market listeners, certification clerks, salvage brokers and fence-side ears, none of whom know whom they report to, assembled through the patron's cutouts. It flags anything genuinely Sith that surfaces behind the light. ([Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md), the doctrine of the open road.)
- **The Ebb** — 45–75 AR: Force-sensitive births thinned to almost nothing, and every tradition has a theology for it. GM truth: the vergence feeding. ([Timeline](../setting/timeline.md); [GM Truths](truths.md), Truth 2.)
- **The Awakening** — the last twenty years: the births rising again; true dreams, the hearth-sense, small luck that bends. The Temple thinks it is ready. GM truth: the vergence leaking. ([The Awakening](../setting/awakening.md); [GM Truths](truths.md).)
- **The Muster (the Last Muster)** — Kaan's dead man's switch: a working in principality stone, keyed to his thread, fired when the thought bomb cut it. It reaches for its master's voice and relays the inside of the bomb instead: a thousand souls grinding at their prison, stone on dry stone. The pull the gifted feel up the Run; the Vigil call it the millstone dream. ([Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md), the Last Muster.)
- **A Civic / Jedi / Sith fragment** — a tangible source or a living witness, tagged by whose history it comes from. Fragments about the same event from different tags always disagree on one load-bearing fact; a relevant fragment adds a boost die to related Knowledge checks; the tags gate the Reconstruction rungs, and rung 3 always needs a Sith-tagged one. ([Lore Fragments](../mechanics/lore-fragments.md); the [Fragment Tracker](tools/fragment-tracker.md).)
- **A truth-ladder rung vs a Sightings rung** — a truth-ladder rung is a Truth paid out by a Reconstruction on one of the four Questions: three per Question, at three, five and seven fragments ([Fragment Tracker](tools/fragment-tracker.md)). A Sightings rung is filed evidence against the Sith line — pattern, hull, face, name — and every filed rung costs the line something ([Faction Clocks](tools/faction-clocks.md), the Ladder of Sightings). One is learned; the other is filed.
- **The Hill (Tarrah Hill, Aquilaris)** — the drowned world's one dry height and its only untaped grave; the stone reads HOLT / Marenna / Kerra / Sisters, cut by Del Gormo, who keeps it and says nothing. The relight turns the Vigil's tending into sealing, and the Hill is hop 2's climax: first and only pass. ([Module 03](modules/03-the-drowned-resort.md); [Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md); [The Dead Lords](npcs/knight-errant.md).)
- **The Annexation** — the Admiralty "secures" a relit corridor for everyone's safety and does not leave. The Admiralty clock's hinge; Reike's "unknown armed actor" finding at Vesta-9 is its founding paperwork. ([Act 2](../campaign/act-2.md); [Faction Clocks](tools/faction-clocks.md); [Module 01](modules/01-the-light-on-vesta-9.md), Scene 6.)
- **Hearth-sense** — one of the Awakening's three signs: reading a room's feeling like weather. In the modules it is the gifted PC's quiet read of a place; on the Hill, run it quiet. ([The Awakening](../setting/awakening.md); [Module 03](modules/03-the-drowned-resort.md).)
