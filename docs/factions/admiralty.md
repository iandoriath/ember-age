# The Admiralty

*The government of the Bastion Worlds — the last power with working capital ships.*

## Who they are

The shipyard cluster where the fleet that refused the demobilization order dropped anchor and never left. A stratocracy built by that squadron — its current Lady Admiral is the granddaughter of the woman who said no — grown into the last power with working capital ships.

The distinction matters on the ground: **Bastioners** are fiercely proud of their worlds and considerably less fond of the uniforms that run them, and the Admiralty works hard to keep outsiders from seeing the difference.

## What they want

The relit lanes placed under naval protection — which is to say under naval control. And they conscript.

## What they offer a wayfinder crew

What no one else can: **escort, fuel, repair, genuine safety.** Their pitch lands because it is half true: the Republic dissolved its fleets by law, adjourned the Rim, and sends paperwork and a Jedi to take it back.

## The crack

Their founding story requires an enemy, and if the Sith do not return, some of their officers will need them to.
