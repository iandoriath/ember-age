# The Crew — GM notes

**GM eyes only.** Per-character material that belongs at the table but not on a player's sheet: what a PC knows that the players should discover in play, and the hooks each sheet hands the campaign. The printable sheets and the Crew tab carry only what a player may read.

## Kinch — the younglings he remembers

Two hundred years as a training droid at the Coruscant Temple (c. 1110–910 BBY) means Kinch sparred a great many of the Order's names as children. Fourteen of them fall inside his window; deeds are from the Legends record (fetched 2026-09-10), the verdicts are his. **Reveal these in play, one at a time, when a name comes up** — never as a list. The player has his tactics and the rules check on his sheet; he does not have this.


| Jedi | At the Temple (approx.) | What they did | What Kinch says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kerra Holt | c. 1042–1035 BBY | Evacuated from Aquilaris Minor as a girl by Vannar Treece; his Padawan; made herself "a living weapon" and went back into the Sith-held worlds alone to fight the Calimondra family. Never came out. | "Arrived at eight with a planet missing. Fought like she meant to get it back by hand. Hit harder than her weight, which is not a phrase I use. She went back into Sith space alone and did not return. I would have gone with her. Nobody asked the droid." |
| Skere Kaan | c. 1060–1050 BBY | Jedi Master who defected around 1010 BBY, founded the Brotherhood of Darkness, ruled the Sith for a decade as "the Dark One," and died with everyone else at Ruusan. | "Brilliant, and brilliant at knowing it. Won every match and looked, every time, as if the match had insulted him. I taught him the overhand. I would like it back." |
| Lord Hoth (Rohlan) | c. 1050–1040 BBY | Joined the Order to end the Sith single-handed; became the Coruscant Temple's battlemaster and Supreme Commander; built the Army of Light from whatever he could find, including Force-sensitive children; died at Ruusan. | "Came in at six wanting to end the Sith with one hand. I recommended two. He never forgave me for being right about the second hand." |
| Pernicar | c. 1060s BBY | A scribe's son sent to the Order against his wishes; Hoth's closest friend and the peacemaker of the Army of Light; had to kill his own fallen Padawan; died at Ruusan. | "Did not want to be here. Best footwork of his class *because* he kept trying to leave the mat. Later killed his own student when the boy fell. I did not teach him that. I taught him the footwork." |
| Valenthyne Farfalla | c. 1050s BBY | Half-Bothan Jedi Lord, famously foppish and vain; brought three hundred followers to Ruusan; survived, and led the Order's hunt for Bane afterward. | "Insisted on being beautiful while losing. Then insisted on being beautiful while winning, which took him longer. The only child who bowed to me after a match. I keep the bow." |
| Johun Othone | c. 1015–1005 BBY | Taken into the Order at ten; Hoth's Padawan, then Farfalla's; fought at Ruusan and lost his master to the thought bomb; later the Chancellor's guard. | "Ten years old — late, for a start — and Hoth's shadow. Blocked like a boy who had already decided the blow was worth taking. Guards a Chancellor now, I hear. Blocking for a living. Consistent." |
| Kiel Charny | c. 1040s BBY | Jedi General of the Republic in the New Sith Wars; Githany's lover; murdered on Ruusan by the initiate Darovit. | "A general's temper at nine. Told me the drill was beneath him. I demonstrated the floor. Murdered by an initiate. I would like a word with the initiate." |
| Githany | c. 1025–1015 BBY | Master Handa's student who defected to Kaan with the Army of Light's battle plans, betraying Charny; fought with a lightwhip; trained the young Bane on Korriban. | "Fast, lovely, and always looking at the exit. I logged it. Nobody reads a droid's logs. She left with the Sith and a lightwhip. The whip is cheating. I have said so in writing." |
| Raskta Lsu | c. 1035–1025 BBY | Echani, named for the legendary Raskta Fenni; spent her whole career on the blade until she was named Weapons Master of the Order; fought Bane in 980 BBY. | "Echani. The only child who walked onto the mat already knowing it was a conversation. We spoke for eleven years. Weapons Master, later. I am not surprised, and I am not modest about it." |
| Sarro Xaj | c. 1015–1005 BBY | Enormous; Raskta Lsu's student; Knighted around 997 BBY and trained to be a Weapon Master after her; died with her against Bane. | "Huge at twelve and kept apologising for it. I explained that mass is a virtue. He stopped apologising and went to study under Lsu. You are welcome, Sarro." |
| Worror Dowmat | c. 1050s BBY | Ithorian healer who never once wielded a lightsaber; carried the Army of Light with battle meditation; served under Hoth in the final campaign. | "Refused, politely, to hit anything, for eight years. Healed the ones I hit instead. I have never been so thoroughly beaten by someone who would not fight." |
| Fae Coven | c. 1030s–1010s BBY | Jenet; Grand Master of the Order after the Ruusan Reformation; wrote *The Jedi Path*, the Temple manual handed from Master to Padawan for generations. | "Took notes during sparring. *Notes.* Became Grand Master and wrote the manual. There is a paragraph on the low guard I taught her. I am cited nowhere. I am cited nowhere in the whole history of the Order." |
| Vannar Treece | c. 1080s BBY | Led the Republic's Outer Rim raids in the Dark Age; lost Aquilaris Minor in 1042 but got one girl out; led Operation Influx and died at Chelloa in 1032. | "Careful, and then careful again. Fought the way he later planned — three steps ahead, one behind. Died at Chelloa. Careful is not armour." |
| Gorlan Palladane | c. 1070s BBY | Left the Order to stay on occupied Chelloa as a relief worker and miner; ran the resistance; disliked fighting; rescued Kerra Holt. | "Would not fight. Would *not*. Sat at the edge of the mat and asked whether I was happy. Stayed on a conquered world to feed people. I have decided that counts." |
| Tho-Mes Drei | undated (a colleague, not a pupil) | Temple instructor known for his lectures on making and keeping promises. | "Lectured on promises. Made me promise never to strike a lecturer. I kept it for a very long time." |


**Hooks.** *Kerra Holt* is the one that matters: the crew climbs Tarrah Hill in module 03 and the name on the stone is a name Kinch taught to block — a player's line for the silent-keeper scene ("I taught her that guard"), which makes the Hill his as much as the pilot's, and the Vigil's warden hears a Temple droid claim a Knight Errant. *Skere Kaan* is the second: the boy who "would like the overhand back" is the man in the holocron (Keepers of the Flame), and Kinch is a Jedi-tag fragment source about him that no Census can count and no Influence can touch. *Hoth recruiting Force-sensitive children* is the campaign's keys-or-children argument ninety years early, remembered by the droid who sparred them. *Fae Coven's manual* makes him a walking copy of *The Jedi Path* — the Temple's doctrine on the Run, in the body of something the Vigil would tape off and the Inheritors would buy.
