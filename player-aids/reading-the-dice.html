1 · Did it work?Successes minus Failures. Ties fail. Nothing below can change this answer.
2 · What else?Advantage minus Threat — a separate currency. It never flips success or failure. Spend it below.
3 · The big onesTriumph (+1 Success) and Despair (+1 Failure): their effects always fire, and never cancel each other.

◆ Out of combat — suggest these

  • Slicing: no trace in the log · one extra file · the backdoor stays open.
  • Mechanics: the repair over-performs · done faster · a spare part salvaged.
  • Astrogation: hours shaved · a landmark for the rutter · arrive positioned right.
  • Negotiation: better price or a favor · learn what they really value · door stays open.
  • Charm / Deception: they repeat your version · you read what they hide · the lie survives one follow-up.
  • Stealth: an exit route found · something overheard · the guards argue about what they saw.

1–2 Adv buys something small; 3+, or a Triumph, buys what the scene turns on. Failed with Advantage? Same menu, smaller portions — it didn't work, and you learned, kept cover, or set up the next try.

◆ Etiquette

  • You propose, the GM disposes. Plain words: "two Advantage — free maneuver to duck behind the crates?" Expect "yes, and."
  • Spend promptly. A decent spend now beats a perfect one after two minutes of menu-reading. Strain recovery is always there.
  • Use it or lose it. Advantage doesn't bank. Shrug, and the GM spends it as scene color — the dice refusing to be boring.

When in doubt: recover strain, boost a friend, or notice something. Never wrong.
