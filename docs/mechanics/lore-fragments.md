# Lore Fragments

*The core subsystem. The players assemble the true history themselves.*

## What a fragment is

A fragment is a **tangible source**: a holocron shard, a rutter, a sealed archive cell, an unwiped pre-war droid memory core — or a **living witness**. The last human eyewitnesses of Ruusan are dying this decade, and longer-lived species still carry the war as personal memory; testimony is a fragment you can argue with, and it contradicts the documents exactly as often as they contradict each other.

Every fragment carries a tag — **Jedi**, **Sith**, or **Civic** — and fragments about the same event from different tags **always disagree on one load-bearing fact.**

Some fragments are not just testimony but **navigation**: a rutter, a war-convoy log, a pilgrim's chart. The deep history of this era is also a *road* — lanes that led somewhere everyone has forgotten — and by the endgame, when the party needs a way off every surviving chart, fragments are what buys it.

## Rules

- **Holding a relevant fragment** adds a boost die to related Knowledge checks.

### Reconstruction

With **three or more fragments** bearing on one named **Question** ("Why did the Jedi leave the Rim?" "What is the Valley?"), the party may attempt a **Hard Knowledge (Lore)** check as a group scene.

- If **all three tags** are represented: upgrade an ability die once.
- If **only one tag** is represented: upgrade the difficulty once — *bias is real.*

### Results

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Success** | The GM reveals a **Truth**, and the party gains an **Insight** — permanently remove a setback die from one defined category of checks, **or** unlock an out-of-career talent purchase the Truth justifies |
| **Triumph** | A secret beyond the Question |
| **Despair** | A **corrupted conclusion** enters play — and the party believes it |

## Named Questions

Keep a public list of the Questions the party is working. Questions open *in play* — Vesta-9's on session one; the call's the night a gifted character goes looking for the sound:

- *Why did the Jedi leave the Rim?*
- *What is the Valley?*
- *Who lit Vesta-9?*
- *Why did the Run stay dark?*
- *What is calling the gifted up the Run?*
- *What did the Adjournment actually adjourn?*

*(GM: track issued fragments and their tags in the GM tools section of this wiki.)*
