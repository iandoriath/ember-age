# The Provisional Republic

*The rump Senate and its Archive bureaus, ninety years into an interregnum they still call temporary.*

## Who they are

They hold unmatched legitimacy, unmatched documentation, a handful of long-lived senators who personally remember the Republic whole — and a writ that ends at the atmosphere.

## What they want

Every relit beacon registered under Republic charter, restoring the galaxy as a matter of continuity rather than conquest.

## What they offer a wayfinder crew

- **Letters of accreditation.**
- **Archive access** — the richest source of **Civic** [lore fragments](../mechanics/lore-fragments.md).
- **The moral cover of the old flag.**

## The crack

Ninety years of "provisional" has bred leaders who prefer custodianship of the idea to the risk of actually governing — the institution has learned to survive by never being tested.
