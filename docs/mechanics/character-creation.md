# Character Creation

## The basics

- **Standard starting XP.** Any career from any of the three core books (Edge of the Empire, Age of Rebellion, Force and Destiny).
- **The crew shares one heirloom ship** — a pre-Ruusan lane-runner, a century old and held together by devotion. This crew's is the YT-1300; the Wayfarer and Ghtroc patterns are there for another table. See [Ships of the Ember Age](ships.md).
- **Everyone takes [Obligation](obligation.md)** — it is a broke, indebted galaxy.
- **Force-sensitive PCs also track Morality.**
- **Duty waits until Act 3**, when the party formally aligns with a founding vision. The three mechanics mirror the three acts: survival, then conscience, then cause.

## Playing one of the Awakened

A Force-sensitive PC enters via the **Force-Sensitive Exile** or **Force-Sensitive Emergent** universal specializations, or **any Force and Destiny career**, at **Force Rating 1 — with no teacher who will have them.** The Temple takes children, not adults; the Jedi who never left the Rim are a rumor; whatever taught you, it was not a Jedi.

Their growth runs through discovery, not tuition: see [The Force in the Ember Age](force.md). Knowledge is treasure.

## Wayfinder crew concepts

The party are **wayfinders** — scout-envoy-archaeologists reopening the galaxy one lane at a time. Any concept that answers "why does this person reopen dead lanes?" works: seekers, traders, diplomats, chartkeepers, salvage crews, interpreters (splintered Basic makes them load-bearing), couriers off the swoop circuit, and perhaps one of the newly Force-sensitive.

## Joining the crew

Three seats are filled and one is open.

- **A Chadra-Fan technician** — the engine room and the slicing.
- **A Republic trooper** — from a garrison that kept its post for three generations after its charter lapsed, and still counts itself on duty.
- **A Dathomiri pilot** — self-taught in the Force, with no teacher and no name for what she does. Sheet pending.
- **The open seat is the face** — the one who talks to certifiers, kings, and wardens, and signs what the crew puts on paper.

**The hull is shared.** The crew's ship is the **YT-1300 Light Freighter** block in [Ships of the Ember Age](ships.md), as printed: six hard points and ninety years of previous owners.

**Obligation** as the core rules print it for a four-PC group (10 each to start; more if you take extra for XP or credits). The book's table outranks this line.

**Somebody carries Knowledge (Lore).** Reconstructions and relic checks run on it, so one seat should have it, and the face is the natural one.
