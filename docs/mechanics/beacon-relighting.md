# Relighting a Beacon

*The set-piece skill challenge. Three stages in any order, built so different PCs shine.*

## The three stages

### 1. Wake it

**Mechanics or Computers** against sixty years of dead-man failsafes.

### 2. Chart it

**Hard Astrogation**, with [fragments](lore-fragments.md) and rutters as boost dice.

### 3. Speak to it

A **Knowledge or social check** to satisfy old Republic handshake protocols — or to convince a guild observer to certify the lighting.

## What a relit beacon does

Each relit beacon:

- **Opens a region** — a corridor of worlds ninety years strange.
- **Shifts the faction map.**
- **Pays a first-lighting fee** (see [the Lamplighters](../factions/lamplighters.md)).
- **Raises the same question: who follows you through?**
