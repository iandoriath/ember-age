## Dial 1 — The line is unbroken, and very far away

A Sith Master and apprentice continue Bane's work — **Darth Veritis** and **Darth Mendac**, patient, hidden, interested in ruins only for what they remember. Their strategy in one line: *every true thing about the Sith, out of the galaxy and into her hands* — the genuine removed, the fakes left on the shelves, so that when anyone goes looking for what the Sith were, they find only what Veritis has left for them. The Run was Sith country in the wars and the Withering sealed its vaults; reunification is their harvest, and the party are their unwitting gardeners. Through the early acts, run them as an **absence** — pattern, never presence — but the absence is doctrine, not plot armor: the players can break it with filed evidence (the Ladder of Sightings), and an earned Act 3 confrontation, up to and including a duel, is on the table. Everything operational — the Patronage, the Weeding, the Scrub, the Census, the culling — is in [Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md).

The campaign's Jedi question is not extinction but *what a Jedi is for* — the Temple's service (Master Seldra Tane, Act 2) or the swamp's (N'Kata Del Gormo, already on Verdanth) — with the withdrawn lineages dead and only their objects left. Both Jedi, the Rade legacy, and the three-Masters codicil are in [Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md).

## Dial 2 — The tide

The Force returns **because it returns**. The Vigil's theology, the Inheritors' doctrine, and the Republic's science are all wrong or unprovable; nobody gets to be right, and the mystery stays a mystery.

The Valley of the Jedi is therefore **not** the engine of the Awakening — it is purely the era's greatest choice. The Force-sensitive dream of it the way compasses find north: it is simply the loudest thing the Force has ever done.

## Dial 3 — Vesta-9 was lit by the Sith

The apprentice lit it — deliberately, per plan, and nothing the Mother would waste a thought on. The line is hunting one object, **the holocron of Naga Sadow**, sealed in some principality vault on the Run (the Bactranate's, under Jutrand, is the catalogue's best guess); the Run had to reopen for the search, and the line could not be seen to open it. Three consequences run the whole campaign: **the Lamplighters are the Sith's scouting arm and do not know it** (the patron's money, the patron's relighting order); **an Inheritor observer filed the Vesta-9 census** on the crew; and **the guild leaks twice** (the patron bought the relighting order; the Inheritors' mole, Chartkeeper Denno Pike, stole it — Act 2's Conclave of Charts is rigged from two directions). The full doctrine — why the Sith open a country they can already fly (discovery, not travel), the two-phase Scrub, the Hutt immunity, the Osk bedside, and the holocron's chapters and Gatekeeper — is in [Keepers of the Flame](npcs/keepers.md).

## Marginalia

**A calendar gift:** Yoda's canonical birth falls in 104 AR — fourteen years after session one. He is not a relic of the old Order; he is downstream of your players. Whatever galaxy they build at the Founding is the one he will be born into — and the Order he someday joins is their legacy.

**Canon is your epilogue, not your cage:** Legends guarantees that a Republic and a Jedi Order stand a thousand years from now, and that Bane's line reaches its final two. It says nothing about what the rebuilding cost, what kind of Republic came first, or whose names history kept. If your players' Founding becomes the seed of the thousand years of peace, the record's silence about them is not a plot hole — it is what recoveries do to their founders.
