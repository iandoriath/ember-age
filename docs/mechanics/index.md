# Mechanics

*FFG Star Wars (Edge of the Empire / Age of Rebellion / Force and Destiny), with the Ember Age's own subsystems on top.*

The three core mechanics mirror the three acts — **survival, then conscience, then cause**:

| Mechanic | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **[Obligation](obligation.md)** | From session zero | It is a broke, indebted galaxy |
| **Morality** | Force-sensitive PCs, from session zero | See [The Force in the Ember Age](force.md) |
| **Duty** | Held in reserve until Act 3 | When the party formally aligns with a founding vision |

## The Ember Age subsystems

- **[Lore Fragments](lore-fragments.md)** — the core subsystem: assemble the true history yourselves.
- **[Relighting a Beacon](beacon-relighting.md)** — the set-piece skill challenge.
- **[The Force in the Ember Age](force.md)** — Wellsprings, witnesses, and Morality without a Council.
- **[Ships of the Ember Age](ships.md)** — the heirloom lane-runner and the hulls of 90 AR.
