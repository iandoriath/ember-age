## Reconstruction payouts — the truth ladder per Question

The fragment rules promise a Truth on a successful Reconstruction, and the campaign's deep secrets have to stay whole — so pay Reconstructions out in rungs. Each Question has three; award the lowest unearned rung, and a Triumph may skip one. (Fragment economy note: the session one–two fragments are all Civic-tagged, which upgrades cross-tag Reconstructions — plan the first **Sith-tag** source early: the principate court seed at Aquilaris is the natural spot, and the Inheritors' recovered census works later.)

- **Who lit Vesta-9?** (1) *The relighting order was written before the relights — someone is choosing.* (2) *The chooser's people fly dark, were at the station before the light, and have flown the Run for ten years.* (3) *The chooser can tell a true relic from a false one — a living tradition, not grave-robbers.*
- **What is the Valley?** (1) *The armies reached Ruusan seven times; the seventh time, both armies died in a day, and the war ended.* (2) *A thousand spirits, light and dark together, are bound in one place — and the dreams about it are true.* (3) *It is not the engine of the Force's return — it is the loudest thing the Force has ever done, and it is waiting for someone to decide what it means.*
- **What did the Adjournment actually adjourn?** (1) *The seats were suspended "until reconnection" — reconnection has happened, and no seats came back.* (2) *The minutes exist; the senators who wrote them are alive, and anxious.* (3) *It adjourned the Republic's obligations, never its claims — the writ was kept alive on purpose, waiting to be re-imposed.*
- **Why did the Jedi leave the Rim?** (1) *They were recalled to one Temple; a few refused and stayed.* (2) *The refusers taught in hiding until the Ebb starved them out — the last lineage ended a generation ago, and left things behind.* (3) *The Temple's rule was written in grief for Ruusan's late-trained dead — it protects children by owning them, and it has no answer for an adult.*
