# Act 2 — The Conclave of Charts (skeleton)

*GM skeleton, not yet a module: it is not on the screen and will not be until it is written scene by scene. Its job now is to be the floor that Act 1's Handoff flags land on, so that nothing the crew wrote on paper between Vesta-9 and Sanrafsix is a promise the campaign cannot keep. Written 2026-08-28 after the editorial review; the venue and the seats are the editor's calls, recorded in the checkpoint for Ian to confirm or change.*

## When and where

**The Conclave sits at Darkknell, when the Dead Gate opens** — when the Run is relit through to the Hydian Way and the galaxy can arrive from the other side. The Lamplighters call it to auction the relighting order for the rest of the sector: the Sanrafsix Corridor, the Enarc Run, the Triellus, the dark interior the Run's spine never touched. Every faction bids; the party holds swing data from Act 1.

**Why Darkknell.** It is the seam: the crew open the door from the dark side, and the Republic's accreditation mission — with **Master Seldra Tane**, the first Jedi of the Temple the Run has seen — comes up the Hydian through it. The Sith-won capital of the Daimanate, its Sanctum Celestial a canonical vergence underfoot ([The Dead Lords](../npcs/knight-errant.md)), is also where *the Awakening goes public* can happen without anyone arranging it. And it keeps the Act 1 shape Ian ruled: the story pulls the crew to Darkknell, and the Conclave is what is waiting there.

## The agenda — item, mover, defender, what the crew hold, what standing does

1. **The sharing precedents** (Aquilaris's stations; Aplooine's dig rights; Sanrafsix's dead, if written). *Moves to strike:* the guild (charting authority) and the Founding cadre (sedition). *Defends:* the worlds, and the Treaty wing quietly. *The crew hold:* the certificates with their names on them, which nobody else can speak to. *Standing:* a Reconstruction on the verdict Question here — **Should the Run be reconnected at all?** — grants the party's verdict the standing of a Truth on the Conclave's founding paper, and every faction clock moves toward or against it.
2. **The provenance-registry clause** (Sanrafsix): the guild as the authority on what is real. *Moves:* Zel. *Defends:* nobody who is not paid to; the Ring's registrar sells to both sides. *The crew hold:* whether they struck, sold or kept their names; Wex's client list, if they took it.
3. **The Vigil's audit**: the seals opened under certified names — Verdanth's two, Sanrafsix's shaft. *Moves:* Tull, reading. *Defends:* the crew, if they choose to; nobody else can. *Standing:* the audit is evidence in the verdict Question's second rung (atrocities and desecrations on both sides of the ledger cannot be reached from one side).
4. **The Hutt creditor's testimony**: Sorulba tells the accreditation mission what the Republic's absence cost, in figures. *Moves:* the mission (it wants a grievance to pay). *Defends:* the Admiralty (it wants the grievance to be *theirs*).
5. **The relighting order itself** — rigged twice. The patron bought it before the Conclave was called (the Patronage's conditions: the sequence is the search); Chartkeeper Denno Pike stole it and sold it to the Inheritors and the House of Seals (the Sanrafsix client-list dates prove lots sold before their relight cleared). *The crew hold:* Denno, if they redeemed him, and the proof that exposes the auction; or a martyr, if they cornered him. Exposing the second rigging never exposes the first; it makes Zel *ask* about the first.
6. **The guild's price.** Zel cannot be bought with scrip, only with permanence: the guild written into the concord. Whoever offers that gets the sequence. The patron already has.
7. **The Temple's seat.** Tane arrives with the mission and asks for two things the Run does not want to give: the sector seat and the children. The codicil — three Masters in a sealed temple may seat a chapter — is the war under the table: the Run holds one unimpeachable Master, one hermit the Temple has doctrinal reasons to deny, and an empty third chair. *The crew hold:* Rade's rutter, if they took it off the bench at Sanrafsix — the Temple's quota in a farmer's cipher — and the crew's gifted adult, whoever the sheet names — the third chair stays an empty seat the Conclave argues over.
8. **The Annexation**, tabled: the Admiralty "secures" a relit corridor for everyone's safety. The Conclave is where it is proposed as *policy*.
9. **The Awakening goes public**: a display too public to hush, in a city built on a vergence, with every government's envoy in the room. Not scheduled. Possible.

## Outcomes the rest of Act 2 reads

- **The order** — the guild's (the patron's), the Republic's, the Admiralty's, or nobody's: which corridors relight next, and therefore which vaults the Scrub reaches last.
- **The precedents** — struck, kept, or bought.
- **The party's verdict** — a Truth with standing on the founding paper, or not written.
- **Tane's stance** — toward the crew's gifted, toward the hermit, toward the third chair.
- **The Vigil's break** — keys or children, in the open.
- **The Annexation** — tabled, passed, or answered.
