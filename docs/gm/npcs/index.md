# NPC Library

*Every stat block in this library is an **official published FFG adversary pulled as printed** and re-skinned for the Ember Age — new name, new face, same numbers. Each block cites its chassis (the printed adversary and book) and declares any mechanical deviation on an `Adjustments:` line (almost always "none"). Nothing here is built on fan homebrew.*

## How to read the blocks — FFG adversary rules in one screen

| | **Minion** | **Rival** | **Nemesis** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strain threshold | **None** — strain damage becomes wounds | **None** — strain suffered becomes wounds | Full strain threshold |
| Skills | **Group only, no ranks** — a group has ranks equal to (members − 1) in its group skills | Ranked, like a PC | Ranked, like a PC |
| Talents | None | Sparingly | Expected — usually **Adversary 1–3** (*upgrade the difficulty of all combat checks targeting them, once per rank*) |
| Dies to | Any hit past its Wound Threshold | Wounds past threshold | Wounds past threshold (crits matter) |

**Minion group math:** a group's wound threshold is the sum of its members' (four WT-5 minions = group WT 20), and one minion drops each time total damage passes a multiple of the individual WT.

**Soak** = Brawn + armor. **Melee damage** is printed as a total (Brawn already included).
