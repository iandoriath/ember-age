# The Republic

*Recovered, solvent, and sure of itself — a thousand lanes away.*

## Who they are

The Galactic Republic of the Reformation: a working Senate on Coruscant, a Chancellor with Jedi peacekeepers at his call, no army by law, and a writ that has stopped, for eighty years, exactly where the relays did. It does not think of itself as provisional. It thinks of itself as *recovered* — and it recovered by adjourning the Rim.

Out here it arrives as charter ships: an envoy-archivist, a cargo of forms, a Jedi, and the quiet assumption that the Run has been waiting.

## What they want

Every relit beacon registered under Republic charter, the adjourned delegations folded into **sector seats** — one senator per sector, not the old one-world-one-vote rolls — and the Temple's quota met: the gifted children of the reconnected worlds, found and sent. Continuity, not conquest — which, to a world that was adjourned without being asked, can look like the same thing.

## What they offer a wayfinder crew

- **Letters of accreditation** — and with them, lanes that are suddenly legal.
- **Archive access** — the richest source of **Civic** [lore fragments](../mechanics/lore-fragments.md), and the only record of the Adjournment's minutes.
- **The flag** — moral cover that still means something, two hops from anywhere that remembers it.

## The crack

A Republic that rebuilt itself without an army and without the Rim now wants the Rim back, and has only two instruments to take it with: paperwork, and the Jedi. Every charter it signs out here is an admission that the Adjournment was a choice — which is why the four worlds relit along the Duros Space Run this past decade, reconnected in exactly the sense the Adjournment's *"until reconnection"* named, heard nothing about their seats for ten years: restoring them one-for-one means explaining why the rest stayed suspended, and diluting every Core vote in the chamber. The overture finally coming with the accreditation mission is the Senate's workaround — **reinstatement by sector**: one senator for the whole Grumani sector, in place of the old one-world-one-seat delegations. Representation restored without the seats being restored, and everyone out here can do that math. It is a hard sell. Everything Republic is a hard sell out here. The senators who remember making the Adjournment are very old, and very anxious that the Rim not read the minutes.
