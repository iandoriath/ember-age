# The Vigil

*Keepers of everything the Order left behind when it withdrew — and the standing peace terms with the bereaved.*

## Who they are

Founded by Ruusan's grave-tenders and the Order's lay custodians in the year of the Adjournment, when the Jedi sealed their Rim temples and went home to Coruscant. The Council deeded them the stone and the relics — **custody of property, though nothing in the minutes grants them custody of the calling** — under a single instruction: keep it locked until the Order returns. An angry galaxy tolerates them precisely because they keep it all locked away; their doctrine is not only theology, it is the standing peace terms with the bereaved.

Their founding generation — the last people who actually knew Jedi — is dying, and doctrine is hardening exactly as fast as memory fails. Eighty years is long enough for *until the Order returns* to become *the Order is not coming back*, and then to become a creed.

## What they want

They gather relics, authenticate artifacts, tend the Valley of the Jedi — and forbid, absolutely, the use of any of it. **Veneration without practice**, so that the fire can never spread again. They are the Rim's own answer to the Force, and they have had a century to decide they prefer it to Coruscant's.

## What they offer a wayfinder crew

- **Sanctuary.**
- **Authentication** of artifacts and [lore fragments](../mechanics/lore-fragments.md).
- **Access to sealed places** no one else may enter — including the temples the Temple now wants back.

## The crack

The newly Force-sensitive split them in half: sacrilege walking around in children's bodies, or the Order reborn. The Order's return splits them again: half the Vigil will hand over the keys and weep with relief; half will not hand over the children, and has begun to wonder whether the deed says it must hand over anything at all.

And beneath it sits the deed itself: their authority covers stone and relic, not flesh — a distinction somebody from Coruscant is eventually going to read aloud, in a temple, with two other Masters present.
