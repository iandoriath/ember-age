# Act 2 — Convergence

*Sessions ~9–16. The quiet ends.*

Hinge events, playable in any order:

## The Awakening goes public

A display of the Force too public to hush — now every government has a policy, and every policy has victims.

## The Conclave of Charts

The [Lamplighters](../factions/lamplighters.md) auction the relighting order. Every faction bids; the party holds swing data from Act 1.

## The Unveiling

The [Inheritors](../factions/inheritors.md) present a trained cadre — the first "Sith" seen since Ruusan. They are costume: real techniques, counterfeit lineage. Almost nobody alive can tell the difference, and the two beings who can have just been handed an address.

## The Vigil breaks

The argument over custody of the Force-sensitive goes from doctrinal to armed, and both halves of [the Vigil](../factions/vigil.md) come to the party for help.

## The Annexation

The [Admiralty](../factions/admiralty.md) "secures" a relit corridor for everyone's safety, and does not leave.
