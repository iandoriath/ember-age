# The Bounty Hunters' Guild

*A standing power — the closest thing the galaxy has to interstellar law.*

## Who they are

When the courts stopped reaching across systems, one writ kept traveling: **the posting.** The Guild honors any bounty whose bond clears — governments, Kajidics, grieving families, it does not care — and its code (*deliver as posted, no poaching, the posting is sacred*) has made it, absurdly, the closest thing the galaxy has to interstellar law.

Worlds too small for judges hire hunters as **circuit arbiters**.

## The dark side of the code

Postings outlive their posters, and Guild purists still honor bounties filed by governments that no longer exist.

## To a wayfinder crew

The Guild is employer, threat, and occasionally the only enforcement a promise has.
