# The Vigil

*Keepers of everything the old orders left behind — and the standing peace terms with the bereaved.*

## Who they are

Founded by Ruusan's grave-tenders and the Jedi Order's lay custodians, deeded the sealed temples and relics by the Council's last act — **custody of property, though nothing in the minutes grants them custody of the calling.** An angry galaxy tolerates them precisely because they keep it all locked away; their doctrine is not only theology, it is the standing peace terms with the bereaved.

Their founding generation — the last people who actually knew Jedi — is dying, and doctrine is hardening exactly as fast as memory fails.

## What they want

They gather relics, authenticate artifacts, tend the Valley of the Jedi — and forbid, absolutely, the use of any of it. **Veneration without practice**, so that the fire can never spread again.

## What they offer a wayfinder crew

- **Sanctuary.**
- **Authentication** of artifacts and [lore fragments](../mechanics/lore-fragments.md).
- **Access to sealed places** no one else may enter.

## The crack

The newly Force-sensitive: half the order sees sacrilege walking around in children's bodies, and half sees the Order reborn and weeps. That argument will not stay doctrinal forever.

And beneath it sits the deed itself: their authority covers stone and relic, not flesh — a distinction somebody is eventually going to read aloud.
