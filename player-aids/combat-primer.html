◆ Getting hit

  • Wounds = damage − Soak (min 0). Stun damage deals strain instead, Soak still applies.
  • Wounds exceed Wound Threshold → knocked out + 1 Critical Injury.
  • Strain exceeds Strain Threshold → knocked out, no crit.
  • Defense: each point = on matching attacks against you (cap 4). Sources that provide defense don't stack — use the highest; effects that increase it (Defensive X) add on top.

◆ Critical injuries

  • Trigger: wounds past threshold · spend Advantage = weapon's Crit rating on a damaging hit · spend a Triumph.
  • Max one crit per hit — extra activations add +10 to its roll.
  • Roll d100: +10 per crit the target already has, +10 per rank of Vicious.

◆ Getting better

  • After the fight: Simple (no purple) Discipline or Cool check — recover 1 strain per Success.
  • Stimpack (a maneuver): heals 5 wounds; each further one today heals 1 less (4, 3, 2, 1, then nothing). Resets tomorrow.
  • Medicine check: heals 1 wound / Success + 1 strain / Advantage. Difficulty: wounds at half threshold or less · over half · past threshold . Treating yourself: +2 difficulty.
  • A night's rest: 1 wound, all strain.

◆ Reading the dice, combat edition

  • Did I hit? 1+ net Success = yes, and every net Success adds +1 damage.
  • Advantage pays even on a miss: recover 1 strain, boost an ally — or bank it into crits & qualities on a hit.
  • Triumph: a Success plus something spectacular — a crit, regardless of cost.
  • Threat / Despair: the GM's currency against you. Full menus: the Reading the Dice sheet.
