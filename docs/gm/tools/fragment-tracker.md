## Reconstruction payouts — the ladder per Question

*(Refocused 2026-08-28 — Ian: the Jedi Question retired (the Sith drove them out; not a mystery), the Adjournment Question reforged into the verdict. Four Questions: three mysteries whose bottoms connect into one road to Ruusan, and one judgment the players make.)* The fragment rules pay a Truth per successful Reconstruction; the deep secrets stay whole by paying in **rungs** — three per Question, rung 1 at three fragments, rung 2 at five (or three with all tags), rung 3 at seven (or four with all tags) **and never without a Sith-tagged fragment**. Award the lowest unearned rung; a Triumph may skip one. Fragment economy: everything placed in sessions one and two is Civic. The first **Sith-tag** sources are the Unbroken Seal's leftover forgery, the principate court at Aquilaris, and — later — the Inheritors' recovered census and the holocron of Lord Kaan's open chapters; the first **Jedi-tag** sources are the canyon dream, the Veshet graves, and Kerra Holt's grave on Aquilaris.

- **Who lit Vesta-9?** *(the hidden hand — absorbs the old "Why did the Run stay dark")* (1) *The relighting order was written before the relights — someone is choosing, and the darkness was convenient for them: every serious push to relight the Run died quietly, and the pickets were ordered not to look.* (2) *The chooser's people fly dark, were at the station before the light, and have flown the Run the whole time it was dark — the Run was kept dark while the old principality vaults were emptied, and the relighting order is the emptying order.* (3) *The chooser can tell a true relic from a false one — a living Sith tradition, not grave-robbers — which means the Sith did not die at Ruusan.*
- **What is calling the gifted up the Run?** *(the road)* (1) *The pull is real and shared: the gifted drift up the Run from every direction; it grinds like stone on dry stone; more people hear it every year — more ears, not a louder call.* (2) *It is a made thing with an author, keyed to fire at its author's death — and it has been calling for exactly ninety years, since the day the last war ended.* (3) *It carries not its author's voice but a thousand others grinding at their prison — and followed far enough it stops being a sound and becomes a route: the lanes to Ruusan that every chart has lost.*
- **What died at Ruusan?** *(the vergence — replaces "What is the Valley")* (1) *The armies reached Ruusan seven times; the seventh time both armies died in one day, not in battle — a weapon of the mind was used, and it took every soul on the field, light and dark together, a thousand strong. Taken, not killed: the canyon dreams are true, and the light in them is people.* (2) *A thousand souls bound in one place is a knot in the Force with the mass of an army, and it has been pulling the Force toward itself for ninety years: the Ebb was it feeding; the Awakening is it leaking. Nobody's theology is right.* (3) *Not everything died there. The weapon's instructions came from one Sith who walked away with a name — Bane — and a rule of two; the knot is a lock as much as a weapon, and whoever decides what to do with it decides what the Force is for the next thousand years.*
- **Should the Run be reconnected at all?** *(the verdict — no hidden answer; the rungs are evidence, and the Reconstruction grants the party's conclusion the standing of a Truth)* (1) *The Republic's abandonment was policy: the cordon, the relays switched off, corporations ordered home, the seats adjourned "until reconnection" — reconnection has happened, and no seats came back.* (2) *Both sides did atrocities here, and the fragments disagree on which was worse: the Sith's cloisters, Chelloa, the Encumbered, Aquilaris drowned twice — and the Republic's Grace Command, a mercy charity that addicted whole populations to an apathy drug as a weapon. This rung cannot be reached from one side of the ledger.* (3) *What survived was what nobody sent: a rotation-state on Syned, the Five Systems, a refugee road through Aquilaris's harvester stations, a kingdom at Enarc, a Jedi who stayed. The Run kept itself alive on its own terms — and reconnection will end that too. Now say whether it should.* **Payout:** the crew's verdict goes on the next founding paper they witness, and every faction clock moves toward or against it.
