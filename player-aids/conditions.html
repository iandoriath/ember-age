◆ Status effects

You are…Which means
StaggeredNo actions this turn — not even trading one for a maneuver. Maneuvers are fine.
ImmobilizedNo maneuvers this turn. Actions are fine (you can still shoot or talk).
Disoriented on every check while it lasts.
IncapacitatedWounds over threshold (plus a crit) or strain over threshold: out of the fight until healed back to the line. Soak still applies to hits on you.

Prone, cover, defense: Ground Combat sheet. Stun damage (strain) ≠ Staggered (no actions).

◆ Healing & recovery

  • Rest. A full night's sleep recovers all strain. A full day of rest heals 1 wound.
  • Medicine check. Difficulty by the patient's current wounds: up to half threshold · over half · over the threshold . Heals 1 wound per Success, 1 strain per Advantage. Treating yourself: . No medpac or sick bay: .
  • Critical injuries heal with a Medicine check at the crit's severity (table below) — one attempt per crit per week. Until then it stays.
  • Stimpacks and the after-fight strain check: Ground Combat sheet.

◆ Carrying too much

  • Encumbrance threshold = 5 + Brawn. Worn armor counts 3 less than carried.
  • Over the threshold: on every check per point over.
  • Over by more than your Brawn: you also lose your free maneuver — every maneuver costs strain.

◆ Obligation — the session roll

  • Session start: the GM rolls d100 against the party's total Obligation. At or under → it triggers, for whoever's range it lands in.
  • Triggered: everyone −1 strain threshold this session; the PC whose Obligation it is, −2.
  • Doubles (11, 22, 33…): effects doubled — −2 and −4 — and the debt comes calling now.

◆ Morality & Conflict (Force users)

  • Using a dark side result on the Force die: flip a Destiny Point, suffer 1 strain per dark pip, gain 1 Conflict per dark pip.
  • Session end: roll d10, subtract Conflict earned. Positive → Morality goes up that much; negative → down.
  • 71+ light-side paragon · 30 or less fallen. Cruelty and lies earn Conflict too; torture and murder, a great deal. No one in this era can tell you what the nightmares mean — except whoever reaches you first.
