# The Force in the Ember Age

*In this era the Force is not a skill; it is an event.*

## Almost no teachers — Wellsprings

Increasing **Force Rating** or opening a **new power tree** requires a **Wellspring** found in play — a functional holocron, a vergence, or, rarest of all, a living mentor from one of the hidden lineages — **in addition to the usual XP.**

Knowledge is treasure.

## Witnesses

Open Force use in front of ordinary people:

- **Shifts their disposition one step** — awe or fear, GM's call.
- **Adds a setback die to social checks with that community** until the party addresses what they saw.

## Morality

Run it as written, but land the aftermath of Conflict harder: there are no counselors, no Council, no one to tell a Force-sensitive what the nightmares mean — **except whoever reached them first.**
