◆ Taking hits

  • Hull trauma = damage − Armor. Past the threshold: crippled + an immediate critical hit.
  • System strain (ion hits, Punch It, extra maneuvers, shields): past the threshold — dead in space until repaired below it.
  • Handling = (positive) or (negative) per point, on every Piloting check.

◆ Speed & range

  • Speed 0 = parked target. Evasive needs Speed 3+.
  • Band changes cost maneuvers by speed: Speed 1 — Close↔Short in 2 · Speed 2–4 — Close↔Short in 1, Short↔Medium in 2 · Speed 5–6 — Close↔Medium in 1.
  • Rule of thumb: the faster ship dictates the chase.
