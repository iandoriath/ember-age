# Act 2 — Convergence

*Sessions ~9–16. The quiet ends.*

Hinge events, playable in any order:

## The Awakening goes public

A display of the Force too public to hush — now every government has a policy, and every policy has victims.

## The Conclave of Charts

The [Lamplighters](../factions/lamplighters.md) auction the relighting order. It sits at **Darkknell**, when the Dead Gate opens and the galaxy can arrive from the other side. Every faction bids; the party holds swing data from Act 1 — and the agenda carries the fights the party started. What the Conclave now carries: the Aquilaris harvester stations, the Aplooine dig rights, the guild's charting-authority clause, the Vigil's audit of every seal opened under a certified name, and a Hutt creditor's testimony to the Republic's accreditation mission. Loudest are the **sharing precedents** (a certificate that named Aquilaris's stations the Seacroppers' property; another that named Aplooine's dig rights the hold's): the guild wants them struck; the Founding cadre calls them sedition; the worlds that got them will not give them back. The Republic's accreditation mission — and with it Master Seldra Tane, the first Jedi of the Temple the Run has seen — arrives for this.

## The Inheritors collect

Whatever they showed the party on Verdanth, the [Inheritors](../factions/inheritors.md) now want paid for in standing.

## The Vigil breaks

The argument over custody of the Force-sensitive goes from doctrinal to armed, and both halves of [the Vigil](../factions/vigil.md) come to the party for help.

## The Annexation

The [Admiralty](../factions/admiralty.md) "secures" a relit corridor for everyone's safety, and does not leave.
