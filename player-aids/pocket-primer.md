# The Wayfinder's Pocket Primer

*Player handout — The Ember Age. Everything your character grew up knowing.*

## Where you live

It is the year **90 AR** — After Ruusan. Ninety years ago the Jedi and the Sith annihilated each other, and the galaxy has spent your whole life quietly coming apart: the HoloNet is dead, the Republic is a rumor with marble halls, and travel between stars is the rarest skill alive. Nobody broke the galaxy — everyone just stopped holding it up.

You are **wayfinders**: part scout, part envoy, part archaeologist. Your crew reopens dead hyperspace lanes one relit beacon at a time — and decides, world by world, what the reconnected galaxy is going to be.

## Eleven words everyone uses

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AR** | After Ruusan — the common calendar |
| **the Withering** | The long decline your grandparents watched |
| **the Awakening** | Force-sensitive children being born again, rising for 20 years |
| **the Temple** | The Jedi — on Coruscant, where they take the gifted young. Nobody out here has seen one in a lifetime |
| **wayfinder** | You: scout-envoy-archaeologist |
| **rutter** | An inherited, annotated route-log — often worth more than the ship |
| **scrip** | Sector currency; nobody's credit is good two systems over |
| **first-lighting** | The fee and the honor for reopening a beacon |
| **the black lanes** | Unregistered Hutt routes that never went dark |
| **a posting** | A Guild bounty — the one writ that still travels |
| **the Quiet** | Deep-rim slang for dead space |

## Things your character knows in their bones

- **Ships are heirlooms.** Nothing is manufactured at scale anymore. Your ship is a century old and held together by devotion. Treat every spare part like a family member.
- **News arrives months old**, by courier packet or swoop racer, already halfway to myth. The swoop season standings are the closest thing to a shared broadcast.
- **A fragment is treasure.** A holocron shard, a page of a rutter, an unwiped droid core, a living witness — history didn't survive in books. It survives in pieces, and the pieces disagree.
- **The Force is a curse word.** Most people have never seen it used. Where it's remembered, it's remembered as the thing that burned the old galaxy down — *Jedi* and *Sith* are one curse in most mouths. If someone at the table uses it openly, everyone who saw will treat the crew differently until it's dealt with.
- **Everyone owes somebody.** It's a broke, indebted galaxy — that's why every PC has an Obligation, and why it keeps calling.

## The people who want things from you

Seven powers are racing to answer "what comes next" — the **Republic** (the flag, back from the Core with forms to sign), the **Jedi Order** (the Temple, asking for your gifted children — none seen out here in a lifetime), the **Admiralty** (the last navy), the **Vigil** (keepers of the sealed relics), the **Inheritors** (whisper-network with forbidden answers), the **Lamplighters** (the beacon guild, your natural patron), and the **Kajidics** (the Hutts, who liked the dark just fine). Above the race sit the **Bounty Hunters' Guild** — closest thing to law — and the **Mandalorian clans** — the finest soldiers alive. All of them will offer your crew work. Most of the campaign is deciding whose coin you can live with.
