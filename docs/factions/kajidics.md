# The Kajidics

*The Hutt clan-corporations — the one interstellar institution the Withering made stronger.*

## Who they are

They never needed the Republic; **scarcity is margin.** Their lanes went dark like everyone else's — but Hutts hold rutters older than the Withering and lifespans long enough to remember every route, so the Kajidics simply never stopped flying. They run the black lanes (the dark, unregistered routes that stayed *theirs*), the fuel and scrap monopolies, and the protection rackets that pass for civic order on a hundred worlds.

## What they want

Their answer to "what comes next" is *nothing, thank you* — they will be paid to keep it that way, because every relit beacon on a route they do not control is competition.

## What they offer a wayfinder crew

What no charter can:

- **Fuel and parts.**
- **Black-lane rutters.**
- **Cash work with no questions attached.**

## The crack

Generational: the young Hutts have run the numbers and think a rebuilt galaxy pays better than scarcity rents ever will, and a clan war over that spreadsheet is one bad season away.
