# The Galaxy at 90 AR

*What daily life looks like ninety years into the Withering.*

## Travel is the defining fact of life

A handful of major lanes still run intermittently; minor routes have decayed past their nav data, and a **rutter** — an inherited, annotated route-log — is often worth more than the ship carrying it. Starships are heirlooms maintained by devotion, and the few shipyards that still function are strategic prizes worth wars.

News moves by courier packet and arrives months old, already halfway to myth. The geography has split in two: the Core recovered — a working Senate, a Temple, lanes that run on schedule — and then stopped, at the line where the relays did. Out past it the Withering is the only weather, and some Rim worlds, forced into self-sufficiency early, have quietly thrived and see no reason to be "reconnected."

## The Force, remembered wrong

In the Core the Jedi are an institution: the Chancellor's peacekeepers, a Temple that sends for your gifted child at three, a word in the newsfeeds. Out here no one has seen a Jedi in a lifetime, and where the Force is remembered at all, it is remembered as the thing that burned the old galaxy down — the Run was Sith country, and the principalities told their subjects for a century who the invaders were. The grudge is generational — a ledger of war dead read out at funerals, with Jedi and Sith entered in the same column. Reactions to the gift run superstitious: awe, terror, and, under both, blame.

*(Mechanically, this is the [witness rule](../mechanics/force.md): in this era the Force is not a skill; it is an event.)*

## The economy of leftovers

The Withering built its own institutions in the corners. Nothing new gets manufactured at scale, so the real economy is salvage: junk barons, chop-yards, and scavenger clans stripping the war's leftovers, with the best finds moving through [Kajidic](../factions/kajidics.md) markets.

Droids are heirlooms like everything else, and a pre-war droid with an unwiped memory core is a walking archive — which makes old droids quietly precious and occasionally hunted. Ninety years of isolation has splintered Basic into local creoles, so protocol droids and flesh-and-blood interpreters are load-bearing professions again.

## The two networks that never stopped running

With the HoloNet dead, news moves through:

- **The cantina** — where a good rumor is worth a drink minimum, and ships still change hands over sabacc.
- **The swoop circuit** — whose racers carry mail, gossip, and contraband between systems. The season standings are the closest thing the galaxy has to a shared broadcast.
