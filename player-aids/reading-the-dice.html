◆ The mirror — Threat & Despair

CostThe GM spends these on you
1 ThrSuffer 1 strain (repeatable).
1 ThrLose a prior maneuver's benefit (your aim, your stance) until retaken.
2 ThrAn opponent takes one immediate free maneuver.
2 Thr to an enemy's next check, or to yours or an ally's.
3 ThrFall prone.
3 ThrUpgrade the difficulty of an ally's next check (→) — yourself included.

DespairOut of ammo — the weapon is dry for the rest of the encounter.
DespairYour weapon or tool is damaged one step (minor → moderate → major).
DespairSomething scene-shaping goes wrong: the reinforcements arrive, the bridge gives way, the contact was a plant.
Threat isn't a second failure. A success with Threat still did the job — it just cost you something.

◆ Out of combat — suggest these

  • Slicing: no trace in the log · one extra file · the backdoor stays open.
  • Mechanics: the repair over-performs · done faster · a spare part salvaged.
  • Astrogation: hours shaved · a landmark for your charts · arrive positioned right.
  • Negotiation: better price or a favor · learn what they really value · door stays open.
  • Charm / Deception: they repeat your version · you read what they hide · the lie survives one follow-up.
  • Stealth: an exit route found · something overheard · the guards argue about what they saw.

1–2 Adv buys something small; 3+, or a Triumph, buys what the scene turns on. Failed with Advantage? Same menu, smaller portions — it didn't work, and you learned, kept cover, or set up the next try.

When in doubt: recover strain, boost a friend, or notice something. Never wrong.

◆ Destiny Points — the table's shared pool

  • Session start: each player rolls . Light pips go to the light side (players') pool, dark pips to the dark side (GM's). It resets every session.
  • Spending flips the token. A light point you spend becomes a dark point the GM can use — and vice versa. Every spend feeds the other side.
  • Upgrade your check: one → on your own roll, or one → on an NPC's. Once per check per side — declare it before rolling.
  • Story edit: a small, plausible fact in your favor — "there's a spare power cell in my kit," "I know a guy on this station." The GM can say no.
  • Some talents and Force users' off-side pips (below) are paid with a flip.

The GM spends dark points the same way against you. A full dark pool is your cue to spend.

◆ Force Points — reading the white die

  • Force rating = how many you roll for a Force power. No blank faces: every die gives light pips (white), dark pips (black), or both.
  • Pips of your side are Force Points. Spend them on the power's basic effect and any upgrades you've bought; unspent ones are lost.
  • Pips of the other side can still be used — at a price. A light-sider using dark pips flips a Destiny Point (light→dark) and suffers 1 strain per dark pip used. Dark-siders mirror this with light pips.
  • Not enough pips? The power just doesn't happen this turn. Committed dice stay out of your pool until released.
