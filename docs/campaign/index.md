# Campaign Structure

Three acts. Three mechanics. Three questions.

| Act | Sessions | Shape | Core mechanic | The question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Act 1 — Reconnection](act-1.md) | ~1–8 | Episodic | **Obligation** (survival) | What did the galaxy become while nobody was looking? |
| [Act 2 — Convergence](act-2.md) | ~9–16 | Hinge events, any order | **Morality** (conscience) | Every government has a policy now — what's yours? |
| [Act 3 — The Founding](act-3.md) | ~17–20+ | Two summits and one canyon | **Duty** (cause) | What comes next — and who decides? |

!!! tip "Players discover this as they play"
    The act summaries below are shareable as the table reaches them. The GM's private notes live elsewhere.
